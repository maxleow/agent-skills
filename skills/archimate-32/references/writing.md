# Simplified writing target

The user requested an approximately 80% application of ASD-STE100 plain writing.
Use that target for explanatory prose and instructions. Preserve exact ArchiMate terms, identifiers, URLs, and code.

This profile uses selected STE principles. It does not claim to implement 80% of the standard's rules or dictionary.
It does not certify full ASD-STE100 compliance.

## Apply the profile

- Use one main instruction or topic per sentence.
- Use the active voice when the actor matters.
- Start instructions with a direct verb.
- Keep procedural sentences at 20 words or fewer where practical.
- Keep descriptive sentences at 25 words or fewer where practical.
- Use the same word for the same concept.
- Keep necessary subjects, verbs, articles, and conditions.
- Write conditions before actions when this makes the action clear.
- Avoid contractions, idioms, figurative language, and vague pronouns.
- Keep technical names intact. Do not replace “Serving” with an informal relationship name.
- Split long explanations. Do not remove information needed for correctness.

During review, aim for at least 80% of prose sentences to meet the length and clarity profile.
Treat tables, headings, formulas, code, and exact source labels separately.
Check meaning and voice manually. A sentence-length result alone cannot establish STE compliance.

| Less clear | Preferred |
